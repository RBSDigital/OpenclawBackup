Buying Rules

  • Prefer right-handed, regular flex, cavity-back/game-improvement irons unless your fit says otherwise.
  • Avoid blade, tour, MB, stiff, X-stiff, missing PW/SW, unclear photos, dented woods, or non-UK/import listings.
  • Ask for close-up photos of faces, grooves, soles, shafts, grips, and driver crowns before buying.
  • Keep GBP 40-80 spare for grips if the clubs are good but worn.
