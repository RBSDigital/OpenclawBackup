## [LRN-20260911-BCG] writing_coach_needs_consulting_playbook

**Logged**: 2026-09-11
**Priority**: high
**Status**: implemented
**Area**: docs | config

### Summary
Editorial coaching instructions alone were insufficient for the requested management-consulting role.

### Details
The BCG-plus specification adds a distinct consulting operating model: named analytical models, evidence separation, options appraisal, requirements engineering, systems modelling, delivery trade-offs, adoption, and benefits realisation.

### Suggested Action
Keep the consulting model library in `CONSULTING_PLAYBOOK.md` and require the coach to select a model, expose evidence gaps, and end analytical work with an explicit recommendation or decision.
