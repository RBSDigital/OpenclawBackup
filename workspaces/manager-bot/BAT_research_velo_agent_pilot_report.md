# AI Agent Pilot Roadmap for VELO Nicotine Pouches

Prepared for: `BAT_research`

Date: 2026-06-10

## Executive Summary

VELO’s public site shows a digitally mature nicotine-pouch business with guided product discovery, strength segmentation, subscriptions, a loyalty layer, newsletter capture, a store locator, and age-gated ecommerce. The most valuable AI agent opportunities are therefore not generic chatbots; they are commercial and operational agents that improve conversion, repeat purchase, compliance throughput, and assortment decisions. VELO’s assortment already spans multiple strengths and flavor families, and the site actively steers new users toward the right range, which makes guided-selling and lifecycle automation the fastest paths to measurable ROI. citeturn0search0

This paper recommends a 90-day pilot focused on three primary use cases:

1. Guided-selling product advisor
2. Lifecycle CRM and loyalty agent
3. Compliance-safe localization and content agent

Two secondary use cases should be validated in parallel:

4. Voice-of-customer and innovation agent
5. Demand, inventory, and promo planning agent

The strategic thesis is simple: AI agents should move VELO from a product catalog model to a personalized, managed consumption model. That shift improves conversion and retention while reducing content and merchandising friction.

## Company Context

VELO positions itself as an adult-only nicotine pouch brand with tobacco-free, smoke-free, vapour-free product positioning, multiple strength ranges, a large flavor assortment, a free-sample hook, subscriptions, and VELO+ rewards. The site also emphasizes store locator functionality, FAQ support, newsletter capture, and an age-gated purchase flow. citeturn0search0

That matters because the business already has the ingredients for a high-leverage agent stack:

- choice complexity across strengths, formats, and flavors
- recurring-consumption economics through subscriptions
- retention mechanics through rewards and email capture
- operational complexity across markets, language variants, and regulated claims

## Prioritized Use Cases

### 1) Guided-Selling Product Advisor

**What it is**

An on-site and post-click advisor that asks a few questions about nicotine experience, preferred strength, flavor preference, and usage occasion, then recommends the best starter path, bundle, or subscription.

**Why it is high impact**

VELO already segments the offer by new, regular, and experienced users, and by different strength ranges. A guided agent removes choice overload, shortens the path to purchase, and can steer buyers toward higher-AOV bundles or subscriptions.

**Business-model impact**

Moves the site from static catalog browsing to personalized commerce. This should improve conversion rate, average order value, and subscription attach.

**Pilot owner**

- E-commerce / CRO lead
- Product marketing manager
- UX research lead
- Compliance reviewer

**Data needed**

- product catalog by strength, flavor, format, and price
- current site search terms and clickstream
- conversion funnel data
- new vs returning customer segments
- historical order mix by bundle and subscription

**KPIs**

- +8% to +15% lift in product-page conversion
- +5% to +10% increase in average order value
- +10% to +20% increase in subscription attach rate on advisor-exposed sessions
- -15% to -25% reduction in bounce from product-selection pages

### 2) Lifecycle CRM and Loyalty Agent

**What it is**

A segmentation and orchestration agent that predicts reorder timing, identifies churn risk, selects the right offer, and triggers email or onsite retention actions tied to VELO+ and subscription behavior.

**Why it is high impact**

The site already has subscription tiers and a loyalty proposition. An agent can coordinate the right message, discount depth, and timing rather than relying on static journeys.

**Business-model impact**

Shifts revenue from one-off transactions toward managed replenishment and higher lifetime value.

**Pilot owner**

- CRM lead
- retention marketing manager
- data science / analytics lead
- lifecycle automation specialist

**Data needed**

- purchase history
- subscription state and tier
- points / loyalty activity
- email engagement data
- churn and reorder intervals
- discount and redemption history

**KPIs**

- +10% to +18% improvement in 60-day repeat purchase rate
- -10% to -20% churn reduction for at-risk subscribers
- +8% to +12% uplift in revenue per recipient for lifecycle campaigns
- +15% reduction in manual campaign setup time

### 3) Compliance-Safe Localization and Content Agent

**What it is**

A content generation and localization agent that drafts country-specific landing pages, FAQs, CRM copy, and product education content while enforcing approved claims, age-gating language, and market-specific guardrails.

**Why it is high impact**

VELO operates in a regulated, adult-only category and presents market-specific shopping experiences. That creates a steady demand for compliant content variants and frequent localization work.

**Business-model impact**

Reduces launch friction and content bottlenecks, allowing faster expansion into new markets or new campaign variants without proportional headcount growth.

**Pilot owner**

- legal / compliance lead
- regional marketing manager
- content operations lead
- localization manager

**Data needed**

- approved claims library
- market-specific policy and age-gating rules
- historical content performance
- tone and style guidelines
- translation memory or approved local copy

**KPIs**

- -30% to -50% reduction in content production cycle time
- -40% reduction in compliance review rework
- +15% faster launch time for new pages or campaigns
- 100% of outputs passing mandatory compliance review before publishing

### 4) Voice-of-Customer and Innovation Agent

**What it is**

A signal-mining agent that ingests reviews, customer support tickets, onsite search logs, and social feedback to surface unmet needs around flavors, strengths, formats, packs, and bundle offers.

**Why it is high impact**

VELO’s assortment is already broad. Growth will increasingly come from better assortment decisions rather than just more content. The agent helps the brand decide what to launch, change, or retire.

**Business-model impact**

Moves product strategy from intuition-led to evidence-led, improving launch hit rate and reducing inventory tied to weak variants.

**Pilot owner**

- insights lead
- product manager
- customer support operations lead
- category manager

**Data needed**

- support transcripts and ticket tags
- onsite search queries
- product ratings and reviews
- campaign feedback
- sales by SKU / flavor / strength

**KPIs**

- weekly insight brief generated without manual analysis
- 20% faster identification of rising demand signals
- +10% improvement in launch decision confidence scoring
- reduction in low-performing variant exposure

### 5) Demand, Inventory, and Promo Planning Agent

**What it is**

A planning agent that forecasts demand by SKU, market, and channel, then recommends replenishment timing, promo depth, and bundle composition.

**Why it is high impact**

A multi-strength, multi-flavor portfolio is vulnerable to stockouts, overstock, and margin erosion from blunt promotions. An agent can optimize the mix continuously.

**Business-model impact**

Improves margin and working capital efficiency while supporting availability and bundle economics.

**Pilot owner**

- supply-chain planning lead
- merchandising manager
- finance business partner
- demand planner

**Data needed**

- historical sales by SKU / market / channel
- promo calendar
- inventory positions and lead times
- stockout history
- margin and discount data

**KPIs**

- -15% reduction in stockouts for pilot SKUs
- -10% reduction in excess inventory on pilot SKUs
- +2 to +4 points improvement in promo margin
- +5% forecast accuracy improvement at SKU level

## 90-Day Pilot Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation and Guardrails (Days 1-15)

**Objectives**

- confirm scope and success metrics
- establish compliance and approval workflow
- assemble data access and instrumentation
- create prompt and policy libraries

**Owners**

- program lead
- analytics lead
- compliance lead
- engineering lead

**Deliverables**

- pilot charter
- KPI baseline dashboard
- approved claims and content policy pack
- data inventory and access matrix
- risk register

**Exit criteria**

- all source systems connected or manually exportable
- compliance approval path documented
- baseline metrics captured for every pilot KPI

### Phase 2: Build the Highest-Value MVPs (Days 16-45)

**Objectives**

- launch guided-selling advisor MVP
- launch lifecycle CRM agent MVP
- draft content/localization workflow for 1 to 2 markets

**Owners**

- e-commerce lead
- CRM lead
- content ops lead
- data scientist

**Deliverables**

- advisor logic and question flow
- first reusable lifecycle segments
- compliant content templates
- test-and-learn dashboard

**Exit criteria**

- internal QA passed
- compliance signoff achieved
- first live traffic or campaign test completed

### Phase 3: Validate, Expand, and Connect (Days 46-75)

**Objectives**

- expand advisor into more product journeys
- automate retention triggers
- introduce VOC reporting
- connect demand insights to merchandising calendar

**Owners**

- CRO lead
- retention lead
- insights lead
- planning lead

**Deliverables**

- A/B test results
- churn-risk scorecard
- VOC weekly insight memo
- SKU-level planning recommendations

**Exit criteria**

- at least one pilot KPI shows statistically meaningful lift or a strong directional win
- business owners agree on scale-up scope

### Phase 4: Readout and Scale Decision (Days 76-90)

**Objectives**

- quantify ROI
- document operating model
- decide scale / iterate / stop for each use case

**Owners**

- program lead
- finance partner
- functional heads
- compliance lead

**Deliverables**

- final pilot report
- ROI model
- scale recommendation by use case
- resourcing plan for next 2 quarters

**Exit criteria**

- board or leadership-ready readout
- approved scale plan for top 2 use cases

## Recommended KPIs by Use Case

For the pilot to be credible, each use case should have one primary KPI and two supporting KPIs.

- Guided-selling: primary = conversion rate; supporting = AOV, subscription attach
- Lifecycle CRM: primary = repeat purchase rate; supporting = churn rate, revenue per recipient
- Compliance localization: primary = content cycle time; supporting = review rework, approved-publish rate
- VOC innovation: primary = insight turnaround time; supporting = launch hit rate, decision confidence
- Demand planning: primary = stockout rate; supporting = forecast accuracy, promo margin

## Operating Model and Owners

The work should be run as a cross-functional pilot, not a pure AI experiment.

- Program owner: growth or digital transformation lead
- Commercial owner: e-commerce / CRM lead
- Content owner: content ops and localization
- Control owner: compliance / legal
- Technical owner: analytics / engineering
- Business sponsor: GM or commercial director

Weekly governance should review:

- KPI movement
- model or prompt failures
- compliance exceptions
- operational blockers
- decisions to keep, kill, or scale

## Key Risks

- Regulatory risk if generated content escapes review
- Over-automation of offers that erodes margin
- Weak data quality in product, loyalty, or inventory systems
- Overfitting pilot results to a small traffic segment
- Adoption risk if teams treat agents as tools instead of workflow changes

Mitigations:

- mandatory human approval for external content
- hard policy constraints in prompts and templates
- tight pilot scope with controlled traffic or audience slices
- baseline and control groups for every test

## Conclusion

The strongest AI-agent opportunities for VELO are the ones that sit closest to revenue and repeat purchase. The first wave should prioritize guided selling, lifecycle CRM, and compliance-safe content localization because they are visible to customers, measurable within 90 days, and closely aligned to VELO’s current digital experience. Once those are working, VELO can extend into VOC and demand planning to improve product strategy and operational efficiency.

In short: the best agent strategy for VELO is not “add a chatbot.” It is to build a coordinated commercial system that helps the right customer buy the right product, at the right time, through the right compliant message.
