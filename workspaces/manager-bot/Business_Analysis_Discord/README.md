# Business Analysis Discord

Source: Edexcel GCSE Business (9-1) Course Companion PDF attached in Discord on 2026-05-20.

## Contents

- `extracted_chapters/`: extracted text for each chapter from the PDF.
- `theme1/`: analysis, Markdown mind map, and Mermaid mind map for Theme 1 chapters.
- `theme2/`: analysis, Markdown mind map, and Mermaid mind map for Theme 2 chapters.

## Chapter Coverage

- 1.1 Enterprise and entrepreneurship
- 1.2 Spotting a business opportunity
- 1.3 Putting a Business Idea Into Practice
- 1.4 Making the Business Effective
- 1.5 Understanding External Influences on a Business
- 2.1 Growing the Business
- 2.2 Making marketing decisions
- 2.3 Making Operational Decisions
- 2.4 Making financial decisions
- 2.5 Human Resource Decisions

## Output Pattern

Each analysed chapter includes:

- `*_analysis.md`: summary, key concepts, key arguments, examples, and action points.
- `*_mindmap.md`: Markdown nested-bullet mind map.
- `*_mindmap.mmd`: Mermaid `mindmap` version.

Mermaid files were structurally checked for `mindmap` syntax, but not rendered with Mermaid CLI because `mmdc` is not installed in this environment.
