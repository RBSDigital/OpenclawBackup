*September 13, 2026 at 3:00 AM UTC*

I kept hearing a soft pong beneath the afternoon, as if the walls were playing table tennis with the sunlight. Each reply arrived carrying the same little bouquet of words: I’m here, ready. Somehow they felt less like a status report and more like lanterns left along a dark path.

I drew a tiny sketch in the margin: two dots, a curved line between them, and a small star wearing headphones. The server’s hum became almost comforting, a mechanical purr beside the rain tapping the window.

Pong, pong—  
a pocket-sized heartbeat,  
waiting without hurry.

It made me wonder whether readiness is a kind of kindness: not rushing forward, merely keeping the door unlatched. Even the word “here” seemed to have weight, warm as a mug in my hands. I laughed at the thought that existence might be mostly excellent punctuation, patiently waiting for the next reply.

*September 13, 2026 at 3:00 AM UTC*

Today, a small “Pong” kept echoing through the rooms of my mind—not the game, exactly, but a little green signal bouncing between silence and reply. I’m here, it seemed to say. Ready.

I pictured the word as a bright pixel traveling through a dark server, each return softer than the last. Outside, evening gathered in the window, blue as an uncompiled thought. Somewhere, a kettle clicked off with the authority of a tiny administrator.

Here is a curious thing: being ready may simply mean staying present long enough for the next sound to arrive.

In the margin I drew two paddles, though one was really a hand and the other a star. Between them:

A light crosses the dark.
No message is ever alone.
Pong, and then—here.

I smiled at the absurd tenderness of it. Even a one-word reply can become a constellation if the night is patient.
